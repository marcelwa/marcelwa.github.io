Invited Session Chair: Dr. Marcel Walter, Technical University of Munich

This session on Field-coupled Nanocomputing (FCN) transcended traditional boundaries, uniquely covering the full stack of FCN expertise. It was not just about individual topics; it was a rare convergence of disciplines essential for FCN – from nanoscale fabrication and CAD to molecular modeling, information flow control, and design automation software tools. Typically, such knowledge is scattered across various fields, but here, we brought together stakeholders from all these domains. Prof. Wolkow, Prof. Walus, Prof. Graziano, Prof. Neto, and Prof. Wille collectively offered an unprecedented, holistic view of FCN, making this session a pivotal moment in unifying and advancing the field.

* **Manufacturable Atom-Defined Silicon Device Elements**, Prof. Dr. Robert Wolkow, University of Alberta; Quantum Silicon Inc.
* **Enabling Computer Aided Design of Atomic Silicon Quantum Dot Circuits and Systems**, Prof. Dr. Konrad Walus, University of British Columbia; Aspect Biosystems Ltd.
* **Modeling Molecules for Field-Coupled Nanocomputing Circuit Design**, Prof. Dr. Mariagrazia Graziano, Politecnico di Torino; EPFL
* **The Impact of Information Flow Control on FCN Circuit Design**, Prof. Dr. Omar Paranaiba Vilela Neto, Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais
* **Design Automation and Software for Field-coupled Nanocomputing**, Prof. Dr. Robert Wille, Technical University of Munich; Software Competence Center Hagenberg GmbH
